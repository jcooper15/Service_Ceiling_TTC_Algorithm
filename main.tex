\subsection{Words to Avoid}
Search for these words/phrases in your document and edit for clarity. By using more precise language, your document will be clearer, and hopefully, more concise as well. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item [$\square$] "done" or "was done"
    \item [$\square$] "being" as in "being done", or "is being done"
    \item [$\square$] "thing"
    \item [$\square$] "going"
    \item[$\square$] "came up with" try "developed" or "created"
    \item [$\square$] "like"  Overuse of "like" can make your document sounds less formal and more "like a valley girl". "Such as" can be a good replacement for "like"
    \item [$\square$] "cheap"  Do you mean quality or cost?  "Inexpensive" is a clearer word when describing costs.  "Poor quality" is clearer than "cheap". 
\end{itemize}
